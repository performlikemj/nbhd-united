The privacy story, told straight

Here's the honest version. Before your messages ever reach the AI, we strip out personal information — that's a core design choice, not an add-on. Recently that shield got overzealous: it started redacting harmless things like workout numbers, treating "benched 225" like it was your home address. A bug could also scramble parts of a message before the assistant saw it, so conversations felt broken. To be clear about what this was not: nothing leaked, and no one's information was exposed. The failure was our own protection being too aggressive and buggy — getting in the way of the experience it exists to protect. We found it, fixed it, and tested the fix against real conversations before shipping it.
